Privacy Policy for Dev/End

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the game Dev/End (the “App”), published by Morkalork (the “Developer”), handles information.

Dev/End is a game. It has no accounts and asks for no personal information. There is nothing to sign up for, and we never ask for your name, your email address or your location.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings, run history and records are stored on your device only, in the app’s own local storage. They are not sent to us, and uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them. Highscores are local: there is no online leaderboard, and the share card is an image you choose to share yourself.

Anonymous analytics and crash reports

To find bugs and to see how the game is actually played, the App sends anonymous events to PostHog, hosted in the European Union. What is sent:

  • Gameplay events: a run starting and ending, a map cleared or failed and why, a life lost and why, an upgrade, certificate or offer picked, an ability or fence type used, a store skipped, a screen opened, a setting changed, the outcome of a two-player session, and the 3D board lowering its quality.
  • Crash reports: uncaught errors, so a crash on a real phone can be fixed.
  • The device details the analytics library attaches by default: operating system, screen size and web view version.

These events carry a random identifier for the installation, not your name, your email or any account. There is no automatic capture of taps or screens, no session recording and no user profile. Your IP address is discarded on arrival and no location is derived from it, so we do not learn your city or country. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Two-player (Pair Programming)

Pair Programming puts one board across two phones in the same room. What the two phones exchange is the game itself: fences drawn, ball positions and the agreement checks that keep both boards identical, plus a random installation identifier and a display name (the App sends “Player”, not a name you provide). No personal information travels over the link.

The phones connect in whichever of these ways works:

  • Directly, with no network at all, over Google Nearby Connections (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct). Nothing leaves the two phones.
  • Directly over your Wi-Fi. To find each other, the phones first swap one short connection blob (network candidates and a certificate fingerprint, nothing about you and nothing about the game). One half travels in the invitation link or QR code shown on screen; the other is left in a mailbox on our server, which holds it in memory for at most two minutes and deletes it as soon as the other phone collects it.
  • Through our server, if your network blocks the two phones from reaching each other, as cafe, hotel and office Wi-Fi usually does. Each phone then holds a connection to our server, which passes the game messages between them unread and unchanged. Nothing is stored: the messages are forwarded and forgotten, and a server restart simply ends the session.

On Android 11 and below, Android requires the location permission before an app may discover nearby devices over Bluetooth. The App asks for it only there, only for pairing, and never reads your location.

No ads, no purchases

The App contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no in-app purchases. Apart from the analytics described above, it plays entirely offline.

Children’s privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your data and deletion

The data on your phone is deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. The analytics events are anonymous and are not linked to you or to any account, so there is no profile of you for us to look up or delete. If you have a question about them, write to us at the address below.
